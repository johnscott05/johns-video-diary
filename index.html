week 15: To Tacoma

Background: A few years ago, I shot some footage on a trip I took with some friends to help another friend move from Salt Lake City to Tacoma for a project that didn't materialize.
As I was looking for something else on my hard drive, I stumbled across this footage. In the moment, while in Tacoma, I sort of gave up on filming for awhile because I thought I wasn't caputring anything interesting.
As I rewatched the footage, though, I realized there may have been something there and, had I kept with it, I might have found it as I continued to film. So for this week, I decided to break one of my made-up rules
for this thing and edit the footage I did have together instead of shooting something new on the camera I have used on all the previous entries. So here it is.
